Admin Inventory Editor Concepts

/invsync edit <player> opens the player's inventory for an operator (level 3) in vanilla chest screens sent by the server, so nobody installs anything. A chest screen is at most 9 slots wide and 6 rows tall, and the viewer's own inventory always sits underneath it; here that part is locked (greyed). Items taken from the list, placed, moved or trashed change nothing until Save, then Confirm. Three layouts of the same parts: the player's inventory (main, hotbar, armour, offhand), the ender chest, the item list, a trash slot, and the buttons.

All options: Save, then Confirm

Save opens this summary in place of the editor. Confirm writes the change (live, or as their newest snapshot when they are offline); Back returns to the editor with the changes still there; Cancel drops them all.

Decisions

  1. Layout. A, B or C. Default: C, tabs and a toolbar: one screen, the trash and Save always in reach, the full item list one click away, and the item on your cursor stays on it when you switch tabs.
  2. Taking from the item list. Default: left-click takes a full stack, right-click takes one, shift-click puts a full stack into the first free slot of the player's inventory, as in the creative inventory.
  3. Trash. Default: clicking the trash slot with an item on the cursor deletes it; nothing is final until Confirm, and Cancel puts everything back.
  4. Shatter Totems (your answer: admins may trash them). A totem the player holds can be moved and trashed like any item; the item list still never offers totems. Default: trashing one is told to BSP-Core (request R8) so its ledger records it as destroyed and never hands it out again.
  5. Empty armour and offhand slots. Default: shown as grey glass panes named Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots, Offhand, which disappear when an item is placed.
  6. Button items. Default: tabs are a chest (Inventory), an ender chest (Ender chest) and a compass (Items); page arrows are arrows; the page number is paper; Trash is a cauldron; Save is lime concrete, Cancel red concrete, Confirm lime concrete, Back yellow concrete.
  7. Search. Default: /invsync edit <player> <word> opens the Items tab filtered to names containing the word; without a word, the list starts at the first creative tab.

Already decided (2026-11-02): vanilla chest screens; edits live when the player is on your server, as their newest snapshot when offline, refused when they are on another server; a change to their data while you edit refuses the save; main inventory, hotbar, armour, offhand and ender chest; every item and block, by creative tab, with search; operator level 3; the snapshot before an edit is pinned, the edit saved as an admin snapshot and logged; your own inventory locked; /invsync edit <player>. Item icons here are coloured tiles with short labels; in game they are the real items.
