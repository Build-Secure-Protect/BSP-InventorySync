Waiting Items Concepts

/invsync give <player> <item> [count] puts what fits straight into the player's inventory. When their inventory is full, the rest waits and the player is asked to make room, then click Deposit; Cancel puts it off until their next login. The mod is server-only, so the "popup" has to be something the server can show on an unmodded client: a vanilla chest screen (A, B) or chat and an on-screen title (C). The player's own inventory sits under a chest screen as always. Here Steve was given 3 stacks of diamonds, a diamond sword and 20 golden apples, and only one stack fitted.

Decisions

  1. Layout. A, B or C. Default: B, three rows: room to show up to 18 waiting stacks at once, a line telling them what to do, and the two buttons apart from each other.
  2. When it opens. Default: as soon as something is left over and the player has no other screen open, is alive, and their data has finished arriving; if a chest or another screen is open, it waits until that closes, never covering it.
  3. Their own inventory while it is open. Default: usable, so they can make room right there (move items, drop them with Q or by dragging out of the screen). Nothing can be put into the popup, and the waiting items cannot be taken one by one: only Deposit moves them.
  4. Deposit when there is still not enough room. Default: what fits goes in, the popup stays open with the rest, and its info line says how many free slots are still needed. When everything is in, it closes and chat says what they received.
  5. Cancel and Esc. Default: closing with Esc counts as Cancel. Chat then says "4 stacks are waiting for you; you will be asked again at your next login."
  6. "Next login". Default: the next time they join the network, not a server switch: a switch through BSP-Teleport does not ask again (it saves as a transfer), so Cancel is not undone within minutes. A Cancel is remembered on every server. A switch through Velocity's own /server looks like a join to this mod and does ask again.
  7. Waiting items are never lost. Default: they stay in the network database until deposited, so a crash, a server switch or a logout with the popup open keeps them waiting. Each stack is taken out of the database just before it goes into the inventory, so it can never arrive twice.

Already decided (2026-11-07): /invsync give <player> <item id> [count] with tab completion and NBT, as vanilla /give; on any server and from the console; operator level 2; straight in when the player is on that server, handed over within a few seconds by the server they are on, or at their next login when offline; Shatter Totems refused; the giver told where it went, the player told what they received, one log line; at most 100 stacks' worth, give.pollSeconds 5. Item icons here are coloured tiles with short labels; in game they are the real items.
