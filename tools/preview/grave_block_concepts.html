Grave Block Concepts

Decided (D25): graves are a block of this mod's own, opened by right-click into the grave screen, and only made for deaths that are not PvP. These are three shapes for that block. Every one uses vanilla textures in its model (no new texture files), comes from a generator script in tools/, cannot be broken, blown up, pushed or burnt, and never exists as an item. The scene: Steve died from a fall; BSP-Core has dropped his Shatter Totem as an item (purple) that places itself after its timer, so the grave goes beside it, never on its spot. Below each scene: the four stone kinds, if the kind follows where the grave stands.

Decisions on this page

  1. Shape. A, B or C. Default: B, an upright headstone: reads as a grave from any side, one simple model, no player skin to fetch.
  2. Stone kind. Default: follows where it stands: blackstone in the Nether, end stone in the End, deepslate below y 0, stone everywhere else. Other choices: one kind everywhere, or a random kind as Simply Graves did.
  3. Facing. Default: the front faces the way the player was facing when they died.
  4. Floating text, drawn by the mod on each player's own screen, so it can differ per viewer. Default: the owner's name; then "Right-click to open" for whoever may open it now, or "Public in 58:12" for everyone else; shown within 32 blocks.
  5. Light. Default: none. Other choice: a faint glow (light level 4) so a grave is easier to find at night or in a cave.
  6. Clients. A block means every player needs the mod (it is in the pack). Default: any version of the mod on the client is accepted, so a server-only fix never forces a pack update; a client without the mod is refused with Forge's usual "missing mods" screen.

Rough isometric sketches; in game the faces carry the real vanilla textures. Sizes are in sixteenths of a block, as in a block model.
