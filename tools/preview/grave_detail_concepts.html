Grave Detail Concepts

Three ways to add glowing golden runes (front and back), the owner's head sticking out of the ground in front, and golden and neon green particles. Each is drawn with the real vanilla textures; the runes are pixel art at the block's own scale (the stone's face is 10 by 11 pixels), exactly what the rune texture would be. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, press Night to see the glow. The head shows Steve's skin; in game it is the owner's own. To make room for a full-size head, options A and C move the headstone to the back of its block.

Decisions (D26, answered 2026-11-08: runes B, head A leaning, particles A)

  1. Runes: the columns of A, the sigil of B or the inscription of C.
  2. Head: A full size and out of the ground to the chin, B smaller and sitting on the grass, C full size and only out to the eyes.
  3. Particles: A drifting motes, B a rising spiral of sparks, C soft wisps.

Mock-ups: the shape, runes and particles are as they would be built; Minecraft's own lighting, its smooth fading of particles and the font of the floating text differ a little in game. Every option needs a new client jar (the exact-version rule), so it goes out with the next version.
