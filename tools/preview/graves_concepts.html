Grave Concepts

BSP-InventorySync takes over from Simply Graves. When a player dies, what would drop goes into a grave instead; the grave is kept in the network database, so every grave a player has, on every server, shows in /grave. Part 1 is how the grave looks in the world; Part 2 is what happens when someone opens it. The scene in every picture: Steve was killed by Alex, so BSP-Core has already placed Steve's Shatter Totem as a block next to the body (purple) before the grave is made. The grave never holds a totem and never takes the totem's spot.

Decisions on this page

  1. Look in the world. A, B or C. Default: B, a gravestone built from display entities: nothing to install on the client, takes no block, so nothing can break, push, burn or replace it and it never blocks the totem.
  2. The floating text. Default: three short lines: "Steve's grave", who may open it now ("Steve and Alex" or "Steve"), and "Public in 58 min" until it is public. Text from the server is the same for everyone looking, so it names who may open it instead of saying "yours" or "not yours". Updated once a minute.
  3. Opening. A, B or C. Default: C, right-click takes everything into the slots it came from, and if anything does not fit the grave screen opens with the rest; sneak and right-click opens the screen without taking anything.
  4. The grave screen. Default: a six-row chest titled with the owner's name; five rows of items (arrows for more pages if a grave ever holds more than 45 stacks), a bottom row with Close (red, left), an info paper (middle) and Take all (lime, right). Stacks can be taken one at a time; nothing can be put in.
  5. What is left. Default: whatever does not fit stays in the grave, in the world or in /grave, until it is taken. Nothing is ever dropped on the ground and nothing is deleted.
  6. The grave goes when its last stack is taken (the gravestone and its text disappear, with the usual block-break puff).

Pictures are rough isometric sketches; in game the blocks have their real textures. Item icons are coloured tiles with short labels. The questions that are not about looks (PvP, how long a grave stays private, old Simply Graves graves, commands) are in the chat.
